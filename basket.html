How to play

A small red lauburu wanders around every page. Grab it, drag it and throw it into the hoop at the right edge of the window. A basket from far away is worth 3 points, a closer one 2. "Far" depends on the window: more than 30% of its diagonal away from the hoop, so it works the same on a phone and on a big screen.

Every basket is added to the shared counter below. Each throw also sends a few anonymous numbers for charts (where it was thrown from, direction, speed and whether it went in). No name, no account and nothing that identifies you is saved. Your own throws are also kept in this browser only, so the charts can highlight them.
