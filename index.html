Latest updates

  • 24 Nov 2026

    Play page: reset and layout

    Five styles in the footer: Archive, Voyager, Metro, Poster (new, between the old Constructivist and Swiss) and Blueprint. Each has its own decoration in the header; Voyager has a planet, a golden record and twinkling stars; Metro shows a real metro map of the site on Home, with Home as the shared station and dashed track for what is still under construction. The Play page cards have a small drawing and no numbers, with the year of the last update. The game now has its own page (Lauburu basket) and Play is an index of games, with a direct entry on the home page. Reset is hidden together with the hoop and the Highlighted/Merged option is gone. How to play moved to the top (justified). The score box has a Reset button for your own score and throws. The charts can highlight your throws or merge them with the rest, the legends fit on one line, the direction radar shows counts on hover, and the regression can be fitted on throws or on bins.

  • 20 Nov 2026

    Play page: charts

    The start-position chart now opens in pixels (with a Window view, a dashed 3-point line and axes), every chart can show only scored or only missed throws, and the radar always tops out at 100%. The Landscape/Portrait filter is gone. The Pixels/Window switch sits between Summary and the start-position chart, which has its own Scored/Missed switch, the dashed 3-point line is removed, and the charts highlight your own throws (kept only in your browser) with a "You" column in the summary.

  • 17 Nov 2026

    Play page and travel map details

    There is a new Play page with a basket counter shared by all visitors; every throw also records anonymous numbers (the card shows 2× and 3× baskets and the percentage scored), and the Play page has charts of them: where throws start, distance, speed, direction and a regression of scoring against distance (where from, direction, speed, scored or not) to make charts later. The charts are now one under the other, with the hoop drawn in the start-position plot, a screen-shape filter, adjustable bins, a count/percent switch, a heat map, a regression card (logistic or linear, with R², odds ratio and more), a radar view for direction, a pixel view of the start positions, percentages of scored vs missed inside each bin, the 3-point rule relative to the window size, speed against distance and accuracy over time. The travel map has no caption now, shows the name of each country when you hover over it, draws Crimea as part of Ukraine, and marks the northern frontier of Western Sahara and Euskal Herria with dotted lines (inside Euskal Herria the hover says so). North Korea has denser stripes, countries are listed alphabetically, only countries I have been to show their name on hover, the Places list was corrected (no Lithuania) and has a new South Korea entry, and the visit counts and city pins are gone.

  • 16 Nov 2026

    About me: travel map

    The Places section now has a detailed, zoomable world map (small states like Monaco, San Marino or Andorra can be seen up close) where the countries I have been to are coloured, with a counter and a row of flags underneath. North Korea is striped (DMZ) because I only saw it from the border.

  • 15 Nov 2026

About this site

I have a background in biomedical engineering and a master's in Data Science and Big Data. Today I work as a data analyst in the humanities sector, where I look at how students develop their competencies over time.

This site is a working notebook more than a CV. Things appear here when they are interesting, not when they are finished.
