About this site

This page is available in English and Basque, and it has no trackers or analytics. The only things it remembers are the style you choose and your basket score, kept in your browser’s local storage (like cookies, but they never leave your device). The basket game also stores each throw anonymously, as explained on its page.

There is a small lauburu wandering around this page. Grab it and try to score in the hoop on the right.
